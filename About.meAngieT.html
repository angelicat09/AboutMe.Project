About Me

Introduction

San Francisco, CA • Dev/Mission Pre-Apprentice

My curiosity began with environmental science and a love for nature. As I grew older, I became intrigued by how our environmental surroundings affect our well-being and our well-being. That curiosity has grown into a passion for understanding, and it has led me through courses in Environmental Engineering, Psychology, Neuroscience, Biology, and Technology.

As a young Chicana from the Mission District in San Francisco, I have luckily experienced being surrounded by others with strong senses of cultural pride. San Francisco contributed to so much of my personality with: farmers markets for fresh produce, resources for my education (like Dev/Mission!), and many public spaces to enjoy the outdoors. As a retired picky eater, I told myself to try everything at least once, leading me to explore different cuisines and cultures. As a first generation college student, I pose the power to understand my culture and city's history, and to create a future for myself full of knowledge and growth.

Sleep & Memory Research – UCSF

Department of Cell and Tissue Biology

Working alongside Master's students, I learned about sleep and memory consolidation through trained conditioning of C. elegans, microscopic worms. Our team removed a protein from the worms' brains and observed prolonged training to test whether they stayed repulsed by a certain smell. Since sleep is so important for memory, we also wanted to know: if the worms are interrupted during sleep, how well can they remember their training?
