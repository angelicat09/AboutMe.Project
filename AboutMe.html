Department of Cell and Tissue Biology-UCSF

Working alongside Masters students, they shared knowledge in regards to sleep and memory consolidation through trained conditioning of C.elegans, or microscopic worms. Our objective as a team was to remove a protein from C.elegans' brains and observe prolonged trainings to test if the worms learned to stay repulsed to a certain smell. Sleep is extemely important for memory consolidation, and as researchers, we wanted to observe other conditions, if they are interrupted during their sleep, how capable are they of remembering their training?
